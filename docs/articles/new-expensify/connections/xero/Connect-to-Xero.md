# Step 1: Connect Expensify to Xero

Follow these steps to integrate Xero with Expensify:

1. Click your profile image or icon in the bottom left menu.
2. Scroll down and select **Workspaces** from the left menu.
3. Choose the workspace you want to connect to Xero.
4. Click **More features** in the left menu.
5. In the **Integrate** section, toggle **Accounting** to enable it.
6. Click **Accounting** in the left menu.
7. Click **Set up** next to Xero.
8. Enter your Xero login details to import settings from Xero into Expensify.

## FAQ

### How do I disconnect Xero from Expensify?

1. Click your profile image or icon in the bottom left menu.
2. Scroll down and select **Workspaces** from the left menu.
3. Choose the workspace you want to disconnect from Xero.
4. Click **Accounting** in the left menu.
5. Click the three-dot menu icon next to Xero and select **Disconnect**.
6. Click **Disconnect** to confirm.

You will no longer see the imported options from Xero.
